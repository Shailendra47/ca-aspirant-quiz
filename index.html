01

Fundamental Principles & Threats to Independence

The Code of Ethics for Chartered Accountants rests upon Five Fundamental Principles that are non-negotiable in all professional engagements:

1. Integrity

Straightforward, honest, and truthful in all professional and business relationships.

2. Objectivity

Not compromising professional judgment due to bias, conflict of interest, or undue influence.

3. Due Care & Competence

Attain and maintain professional knowledge; act diligently according to technical standards.

4. Confidentiality

Respect confidential client data; no disclosure without specific authorization or legal duty.

5. Professional Behaviour

Comply with laws and avoid any action that discredits or brings disrepute to the profession.

Five Threats to Independence (Mnemonic: SAFIS):

  • Self-Interest Threat: Financial or other interest inappropriately influencing judgment (e.g. holding shares in audit client).
  • Self-Review Threat: Evaluating results of a previous judgment made or other services provided by oneself.
  • Advocacy Threat: Promoting a client's position to the point where objectivity is compromised.
  • Familiarity Threat: Long or close relationship creating undue sympathy toward client's interests.
  • Intimidation Threat: Being deterred from acting objectively by actual or perceived threats.
Concept of Safeguards: Controls created by legislation, professional regulation, or internal firm procedures that eliminate threats or reduce them to an acceptable level.
02

CA in Practice & KYC (Know Your Client) Norms

Under Section 2(2) of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, a member is deemed to be in practice when, individually or in partnership, they offer to perform or perform audit, verification, financial representations, or services rendered to the public, regardless of whether a formal sole proprietorship has been registered.

Mandatory KYC Norms: Council guidelines mandate that members in practice verify client identity, address, and source of business to protect the member and the public against money laundering, terror financing, and illicit activities.

03

Schedules to the Act (Misconduct Framework)

First Schedule

Deals primarily with Members in Practice (Parts I, II, III, IV). Covers soliciting, sharing fees, accepting unpermitted work, negligence, and non-compliance with Council regulations. Inquiries handled by the Board of Discipline.

Second Schedule

Deals with Members in Employment and more severe breaches across all members (gross negligence, certifying without examination, disclosing confidential data without duty). Inquiries handled by the Disciplinary Committee.

04

Disciplinary Mechanism & Ethical Standards Board (ESB)

The Ethical Standards Board (ESB) is a statutory standing committee of the ICAI that issues official interpretations, advisories, and decisions regarding the Code of Ethics. Its rulings serve as direct precedents for exam problems involving conflict of interest, advertising, auditor rotation, and concurrent appointments.

ICAI Motto: "Ya Esha Supteshu Jagarti" (Kathopanishad) — "He who is awake in those that sleep." Inspires the auditor's duty of eternal vigilance for public trust.
